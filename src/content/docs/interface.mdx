<DocSection title="Two Views">

The **grid** shows every glyph in the font as a cell. It is where you
browse, search, filter, select several glyphs at once, and apply marks.

The **editor** shows one glyph with its points, handles, and metrics.
Its neighbours are drawn around it, so you always see the glyph in
context rather than alone on a page.

Double-click a cell to enter the editor. The tab strip above the
canvas holds several edit sessions at once. Press the plus to open
another session on the current glyph. Each session keeps its own
buffer, tool, selection, viewport, and undo stack, and follows a glyph
by name across renames and master switches.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Left Panel">

<table>
  <caption>Left panel</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Search</th><td>Filter by name or Unicode value. The buttons beside the box set the scope and turn on regular expressions and case matching.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Categories</th><td>Unicode categories: letters, marks, numbers, punctuation, symbols.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Languages</th><td>Language and script rows with Google Fonts coverage. A row missing glyphs shows a plus that adds them, named and encoded, to every master.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Filters</th><td>Saved filters. A filter you build from the search controls can be kept and reused; they are stored in the font.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Sort</th><td>Order the grid by name or by Unicode value.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Right Panel">

The right panel changes with what is selected and which tool is
active. Sections appear and disappear rather than greying out.

<table>
  <caption>Right panel sections</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Preview</th><td>The current glyph drawn large, with an anatomy view: outline, handle lines, points, and a metric frame.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Glyph</th><td>Name, Unicode value, and left and right kerning groups. Editing the name updates components in other glyphs, group membership, kerning pairs, and the open text session.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Selection</th><td>Coordinates for what is selected, a nine-point reference picker, and numeric move and scale fields. Works on points, a component, or an anchor.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Masters</th><td>One row per master, each showing the current glyph as that master draws it. Axis sliders sit above.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Background</th><td>Show, send, swap, or clear the background layer, and name a reference glyph to ghost behind the drawing.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Measure</th><td>Appears with the measure tool. Toggles for outline colouring, handle and segment lengths, stems and counters, side bearings, and sums.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Curves</th><td>Curvature comb and continuity display.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Transformations</th><td>Flip, rotate, and duplicate tiles.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Bottom Bar">

The bottom bar carries the metrics of the current glyph: advance
width, left and right side bearings, and the current master. Editing a
field moves the outline or the sidebearing directly.

In the grid, the bottom bar shows the current selection and offers to
copy it as text.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Header">

The header shows the path of the open font and the state of the file.
After a save it reads the time, so you can tell at a glance whether
what is on screen is on disk.

</DocSection>
