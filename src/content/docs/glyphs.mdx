<DocSection title="Add and Remove">

A language row in the left panel that is missing glyphs shows a plus.
Pressing it adds every missing glyph for that language, named and
encoded, to every master at once.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Name, Unicode, Groups">

The Glyph section of the right panel holds the name, the Unicode
value, and the left and right kerning groups.

Unicode values are accepted as `0041`, `U+0041`, or `0x41`.

Renaming updates every place the old name was used: components in
other glyphs, kerning group membership, kerning pairs, the open text
session, and the current selection. Kerning group edits are written to
`groups.plist` for every master, and clearing a field removes the
membership.

<Callout>
  Renaming and Unicode editing need the native application. Text
  fields cannot take focus in the browser build yet.
</Callout>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Marks and Selection">

Cells in the grid take colour marks. Cmd-click toggles a cell,
shift-click selects a range in the order shown, and a mark applies to
the whole selection. The footer copies the selection as text.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Metrics">

Advance width and side bearings live in the bottom bar. Editing a
field moves the outline or the sidebearing. Glyph, Update Metrics
recalculates them, and <code>Cmd+Ctrl+M</code> does the same.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Images">

<table>
  <caption>Image commands</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Place Image…</th><td>Puts a bitmap behind the glyph as a reference.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Trace Image…</th><td>Converts the placed bitmap into contours and replaces what the glyph holds. Undoable.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Remove Image</th><td>Takes the bitmap away.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Import SVG…</th><td>Brings an SVG outline in as contours.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Export Glyph as SVG</th><td>Writes the current glyph out as an SVG file.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

The Background section of the right panel is separate from a placed
image. It shows, sends to, swaps with, or clears the UFO
`public.background` layer, which is saved with the font. It can also
ghost another glyph behind the drawing, named by hand.

</DocSection>
