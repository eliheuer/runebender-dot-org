<DocSection title="Select">

<table>
  <caption>Selecting</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Click</th><td>Select a point, a handle, a component, or an anchor.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Shift-click</th><td>Add a point to the selection, or remove it.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Drag on empty canvas</th><td>Marquee.</td></tr>
    <tr><th><code>Cmd+A</code> / <code>Cmd+Alt+A</code></th><td>Select or deselect every point.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Tab / Shift+Tab</th><td>Step the selection through the glyph's points in contour order.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<table>
  <caption>Moving</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Drag a point</th><td>Move it. A handle on a smooth point travels with it.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Drag a handle</th><td>Reshape the segment.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Arrow keys</th><td>Nudge. Hold Shift for a larger step, Shift+Cmd for the largest.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Drag a sidebearing edge</th><td>Change spacing without touching the outline.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Double-click a point</th><td>Switch an on-curve point between corner and smooth.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Alt-click a segment</th><td>Give the segment two curve handles.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Double-click a component</th><td>Edit its base glyph in place, inside this glyph. Escape, or a double-click on empty space, goes back.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

For an exact move or scale, use the Coordinates panel. Pick a reference
point on the nine-point grid. X and Y move the selection so the
reference lands there; W and H scale about it.

The Lasso tool shares the Select button. Hold the button to pick it.
Drag a free shape around the points to select them. Hold Shift to add
to the selection.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Hand">

Drag to move the view. The glyph and the selection do not change.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Pen">

Click to place a corner point. Click and drag to place a point and
pull its outgoing handle out of it. Click the first point of the
contour to close it.

Leave a contour open and it stays open. That is the starting point for
the monoline workflow: draw open skeletons, then type a width in the
Stroke width field of the Path Operations section. See
[Paths and Filters](/docs/paths.html).

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Hyperbezier Pen">

Draws hyperbezier contours: curves defined by the points you place,
with the control points solved rather than positioned by hand. Fewer
things to hold, and the curvature stays continuous by construction.
Alt-click places a corner.

Path → Hyperbezier to Cubic converts a hyperbezier contour into
ordinary cubic segments. With nothing selected it converts every
hyperbezier contour in the glyph. Do that before handing a source to a
tool that expects plain cubics.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Shapes">

Rectangle and Ellipse: drag a box and the shape fills it. The Shapes
button also holds Metaball and Local AI Shapes. Hold the button to
pick one.

Metaball: click to place blending centers. See
[Metaballs](/docs/metaballs.html).

Local AI Shapes: drag a box and type a word. A model trained on your
own drawings fills the box.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Knife">

Drag a line across the outline to cut it.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Measure">

The measure tool draws labels over the glyph. Picking the tool turns
on handle lengths, segment lengths, side bearings and popcount sums.

The Measure section of the right panel holds the toggles.

<table>
  <caption>Measure toggles</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Color</th><td>Tints the outline by thickness, so uneven weight is visible rather than inferred.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Handles</th><td>Labels every handle.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Segments</th><td>Labels every segment.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Bearings</th><td>Shows the side bearings on the canvas.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Popcount sums</th><td>Writes each length as a sum of powers of two, so 96 reads as 64+32.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

The View menu has the same overlays, and two more: Segment Sizes and
Stems & Counters. All On and All Off set them all at once.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Text">

Type, and the font lays out as real text with kerning applied. Click a
glyph in the line to edit it in place, with its neighbours around it.

Text handles bidirectional scripts. Arrow keys move the caret
visually, so left means left on screen in Arabic and Hebrew. See
[Text and Shaping](/docs/text.html).

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Label and Sketch">

Label: for neural fonts. Say which ink of a drawing belongs to each
letter of its text.

Sketch: draw temporary raster ink, separate from the glyph's outline.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Preview">

There is no preview tool. Hold Space with any tool to see the filled
glyph without points, handles, or guides. Drag while you hold it to
pan. Release Space to go back.

</DocSection>
