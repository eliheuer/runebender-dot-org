<DocSection title="Select">

Drag a point to move it. Drag a handle to reshape the curve. Drag on
empty canvas for a marquee. Shift extends the selection.

Arrow keys nudge the selection. Hold a modifier for the larger steps.

Drag the sidebearing edges to change spacing without touching the
outline. Drag a component to move it, and double-click it to open the
glyph it points at.

<code>Cmd+A</code> selects every point, <code>Cmd+Alt+A</code> deselects.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Pen">

Click to place a corner point. Click and drag to place a point and
pull its outgoing handle out of it. Click the first point of the
contour to close it.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Hyperbezier Pen">

Draws hyperbezier contours: curves defined by the points you place
rather than by handles you position. Use Path, Hyperbezier to Cubic to
convert a hyperbezier contour into ordinary cubic segments. With
nothing selected, that command converts every hyperbezier contour in
the glyph.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Shapes">

Drag a rectangle or an ellipse. Hold shift to constrain it to a square
or a circle.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Knife">

Drag a line across the outline to cut it. Hold shift to lock the cut
to an axis.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Measure">

Drag a line to measure across the glyph. Hold shift to lock it to an
axis. The Measure section in the right panel turns on outline
colouring, handle lengths, segment lengths, stems and counters, side
bearings, and running sums.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Text">

Type, and the font lays out as real text with kerning applied. Click a
glyph in the line to edit it in place. Editing changes propagate to
the line as you work.

Text handles bidirectional scripts. Arrow keys move the caret
visually, not by storage order, so left means left on screen in Arabic
and Hebrew. See [Text and Shaping](/docs/text.html).

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Preview">

Hides points, handles, and metric guides so you see the shapes alone.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Shift">

<Callout>
  Shift constrains the shapes, knife, and measure tools. It does not
  constrain point drags: there it extends the selection.
</Callout>

</DocSection>
