<DocSection title="Menus Without Shortcuts">

<Callout>
  Everything in the menus works whether or not it has a key. The Path
  and Filter menus hold many operations that do not: booleans,
  extremes, rounding, rotation, harmonize, balance, optimize, curve
  and metaball conversion, offset, extrude, roughen, round corners,
  and slant.
</Callout>
