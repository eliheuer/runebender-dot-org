<DocSection title="Menus Without Shortcuts">

<Callout>
  Everything in the menus works whether or not it has a key. The Path
  and Filter menus hold many operations that do not: booleans,
  extremes, rounding, harmonize, balance, optimize, curve conversion,
  offset, extrude, roughen, round corners, and slant.
</Callout>

In the browser build, in-window menu items do not activate yet. Use
the keyboard commands there.

</DocSection>
