<DocSection title="Clean Up">

<table>
  <caption>Path menu, cleanup</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Tidy Up Paths</th><td><code>Cmd+Shift+T</code>. Drops zero-length segments.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Add Extremes</th><td>Inserts points at the extremes of each curve.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Round Coordinates</th><td>Snaps every coordinate to whole units.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Correct Path Direction</th><td><code>Cmd+Shift+R</code>. Sets outer contours counter-clockwise and holes clockwise, by containment depth.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Reverse Contours</th><td><code>Cmd+Alt+Shift+R</code>. Flips direction by hand.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Set Start Point</th><td>Makes the selected point the first point of its contour.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Combine">

<table>
  <caption>Boolean operations</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Remove Overlap</th><td><code>Cmd+Shift+O</code>. Merges overlapping contours into one outline.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Union</th><td>Adds the selected contours together.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Subtract</th><td>Cuts the later contours out of the first.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Intersect</th><td>Keeps only the area they share.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Exclude</th><td>Keeps only the area they do not share.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Transform">

<table>
  <caption>Transformations</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Flip Horizontal</th><td><code>Cmd+Shift+H</code></td></tr>
    <tr><th>Flip Vertical</th><td><code>Cmd+Shift+V</code></td></tr>
    <tr><th>Rotate</th><td>90 degrees left, 90 degrees right, or 180 degrees.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Duplicate Selection</th><td><code>Cmd+D</code>. Copies contours, components, and anchors.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Duplicate + Repeat</th><td><code>Cmd+Alt+Shift+T</code>. Duplicates and re-applies the last flip or rotation.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

For an exact move or scale, use the Selection section of the right
panel. Pick a reference point on the nine-point grid, then set X and Y
to move the selection so the reference lands there, or W and H to
scale about it.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Curve Quality">

<table>
  <caption>Curve commands</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Harmonize</th><td>Makes the curvature continuous across an on-curve point.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Balance</th><td>Evens out the handles of a segment.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Optimize</th><td>Reduces the point count where the shape allows it.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

The Curves section of the right panel draws a curvature comb and a
continuity display, so you can see the result rather than guess at it.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Convert">

<table>
  <caption>Curve type</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Hyperbezier to Cubic</th><td>Converts hyperbezier contours to ordinary cubic segments.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Quadratic to Cubic</th><td>For sources that came from TrueType outlines.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Cubic to Quadratic</th><td>For output that needs them.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Filters">

Filters reshape an outline rather than clean it up.

<table>
  <caption>Filter menu</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Offset Curve</th><td>Grows or shrinks the outline by a distance.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Extrude</th><td>Pushes the shape out along a direction.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Roughen</th><td>Breaks smooth edges into a rougher line.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Round Corners</th><td>Fillets the selected corners. The size and handle ratio follow the corners already in the glyph.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Slanter</th><td>Slants the outline, for oblique work.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Context Menu">

Right-click on the canvas for the operations that apply to what is
under the pointer: lock and unlock a component, decompose one or all,
add a component, set the start point, reverse the contour, round
corners, move a contour up or down in the order, and add or delete an
anchor.

</DocSection>
