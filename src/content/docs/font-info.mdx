<DocSection title="Where It Is">

The Font Info section appears in the right panel in the grid view. It
edits the active master and writes to that master's
`fontinfo.plist`.

Switch masters in the Masters section to edit another one.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Names">

<table>
  <caption>Names</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Family Name</th><td>The family every style belongs to.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Style Name</th><td>This master's style.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>UPM</th><td>Units per em. The coordinate grid everything else is measured in.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<Callout>
  Change UPM only at the start of a project. Every coordinate in the
  font is expressed in these units, and changing it later does not
  rescale the drawings.
</Callout>

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Vertical Metrics">

<table>
  <caption>Vertical metrics</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Ascender, Descender</th><td>The UFO values, used by the editor and by tools reading the source.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Cap Height</th><td>The height of the capitals.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Italic Angle</th><td>The slant, in degrees.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>typoAsc, typoDesc, typoGap</th><td>The OS/2 typographic values.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>hheaAsc, hheaDesc, hheaGap</th><td>The hhea values.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>winAsc, winDesc</th><td>The OS/2 Windows values, which govern clipping.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

Three sets of vertical metrics exist because different systems read
different ones. They need to agree, or a line of text sits at
different heights in different applications.

The Compare section reports vertical metrics that disagree between
masters, which is the other way they go wrong.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Hinting Values">

<table>
  <caption>PostScript values</caption>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th>Blue Values, Other Blues</th><td>The alignment zones: baseline, x-height, cap height, and the overshoots around them.</td></tr>
    <tr><th>Stems V, Stems H</th><td>The dominant stem widths, vertical and horizontal.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

The Dimensions section measures the stems actually present in your
reference glyphs, per master. Use it to check that the values you
typed here match what you drew.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Axes and Instances">

Axes, instances, and axis mappings belong to the designspace rather
than to one master, so they live in the Axes section. See
[Masters and Interpolation](/docs/masters.html).

</DocSection>
