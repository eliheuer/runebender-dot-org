<DocSection title="Open a Font">

<Callout>
  Work on a copy. Runebender writes to your source files in place.
</Callout>

1. Start the editor with a path, or use File, Open.
2. Point it at a `.designspace` file, a `.ufo` directory, or a folder
   that holds them.
3. The glyph grid fills the window.

A designspace opens every master it names. A single UFO opens as one
master.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Find a Glyph">

The left panel lists categories, languages, and saved filters. Type in
the search box to narrow the grid by name or by Unicode value. The
buttons beside the box switch the search between name, Unicode, and
everything, and turn on regular expressions or case matching.

Double-click a cell to open that glyph.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Draw">

Press the pen tool in the toolbar. Click to place corner points. Click
and drag to place a point and pull its outgoing handle. Click the
first point again to close the contour.

Switch to the select tool to move points. Drag a point to move it,
drag a handle to reshape the curve, and drag a marquee to select
several points at once.

Press <code>Cmd+Z</code> to undo. Every edit session keeps its own
undo stack.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="See It in Text">

Press the text tool. Type, and the glyphs of the current font lay out
in a line with real kerning. Click a glyph in that line to edit it in
place, with its neighbours still visible around it.

</DocSection>

<DocSection title="Save">

Press <code>Cmd+S</code>. Runebender writes the open UFO or
designspace back to disk and shows the time in the header.

</DocSection>
